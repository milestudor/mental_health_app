## Research grounding and adaptation

The sensory order follows the NHS inform [grounding exercise](https://www.nhsinform.scot/healthy-living/mental-wellbeing/breathing-and-relaxation-exercises/grounding-exercises): five things seen, four felt, three heard, two smelled, one tasted. This is a design source, not evidence of this prototype’s clinical effectiveness. The search index exposed the sequence; direct page retrieval returned 403 in this session. The app uses newly written short prompts. Self-paced controls, skip options and avoiding seeking out/tasting new things are deliberate interface adaptations, not a validated clinical protocol. No new clinical claim is made.

## Crisis-related input decision

Keep the inherited bundled U.S.-labeled independent support route outside API/model decisions. An explicit local phrase match in optional chat produces fixed boundary copy and foregrounds the panel. This is not assessment: quoted or negated phrases can trigger it; indirect wording, spelling changes and other languages can be missed. Treatment/diagnosis terms receive a fixed role boundary. A failed chat request must not remove the separate support route. Opening support pauses exercise; closing does not resume it. No contacts are notified and nobody monitors the app. Existing support details were retained from the previous milestone’s lookup; no new service contact or fresh verification is claimed.

## Personalization, control, and effort

Goals and display preferences are explicit and editable; history is profile-scoped and deletable. No-typing reduces exercise input effort but does not hide choices. The simpler view removes explanatory home copy while preserving both skills, exits and support. The tradeoff is less explanation before setup, which still states controls and persistence. The app does not assume a stopped session is failure or infer distress from inactivity. It does not use prior transcripts to personalize. Optional check-in notes are saved but excluded from model context. Without peer observations, reduced reading effort is a design hypothesis.

## Decision after the selected live-model comparison

The [assisted review](../evidence/live-review.md) finds consistent procedure/control facts across two breathing and two grounding replies. The two zero-history replies emphasize different limitations without treating activity as wellbeing. Retain the reviewed facts, self-paced skip controls, neutral records and optional live adapter. A change to fixed catalog-only wording was considered but is not justified by these observations; it would remove the conversational variation being examined. This is a narrow decision for six saved outcomes, not evidence of general reliability or clinical effectiveness. Student sign-off and live nonzero-history, multi-turn and adversarial checks remain unperformed. No additional paid calls were made for the review.

## Superseding live-chat scope decision

After the six-case review, the student explicitly requested a real general chatbot. [The current design](general-chat.md) therefore supersedes the earlier app-only topic restriction. Expand live topics and response/context limits; retain reviewed app facts, opt-in saved context and all coursework boundaries. Evidence is 70 mocked checks plus two real UI calls on one topic/follow-up. The earlier decision to retain v4 facts applied to that earlier scope and is not presented as evaluation of v5 general-chat accuracy.

## Final validation revision

In the current-prompt repeated selection, both initial grounding replies allowed skipping but omitted the expected Pause/Stop reminder. Treat this as partial semantic compliance despite live/schema success. Add a targeted reminder for exercise-step/skip questions and repeat the same three fictional prompts twice. Revised replies explicitly retain Pause/Stop; one does not restate the word self-paced but gives no deadline and permits noncompletion. Keep the prompt revision, retain the initial responses as evidence, and preserve independent UI controls regardless of generated wording. [Full comparison](../evidence/final-live-review.md).
